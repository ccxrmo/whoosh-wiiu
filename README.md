# Whoosh for Wii U
> [!NOTE] 
> HTML Prototype for a version of Whoosh for the Wii U

## Concept
> "*When you first boot up the app you’ll be met with a greeting screen, people need to have a Whoosh account to use the app and they CANNOT make an account on the Wii U, so they need to go to the website and have an account made there first because Whoosh Wii U is more of an extension of Whoosh and its not the normal service you would have in your phone or in your web browser, that’s why you need an account first. Your Whoosh account then can be connected to your Pretendo Network ID so you can see if people that have a Wii U are on the service by checking your friend list and the list of the people you’ve met while playing online. Then you’ll be faced with a screen saying that “This app supports SpotPass, by activating SpotPass you can receive exclusive content blah blah blah” and it will list and show everything that our SpotPass server gives to the user. Then you’ll be faced with a screen that will make you choose the Mii you want to use, you can either use the one that represents your profile or make one or choose another me, either way it will take you to the mii editor where you can add clothes and accessories to the mii as if it was tomodachi life or fucking reddit. Then you’ll choose a Theme through the list of the initial themes offered and bundled with the app, and BOOM you’ll see the menu and you’ll start using Whoosh.*"

The functionalities exclusive to Whoosh Wii U are intended to extend to:
- Handwritten Posts and the use of stamps from all the games you’ve played fetching the save data.
- Adding people to the Wii U Friend List through Whoosh.
- Having special stamps that are obtained by posting and engages (received or sent)
- A currency obtained obviously through engages and posting that can let you buy more accessories and themes. *"Like Flipnote Studio in a way."*

> *"SpotPass will be used to send notifications through the applet to alert the user about new posts and new engages, the user will not receive notifications in bulk but they will receive a - notification daily or weekly or even monthly based on what the user chooses to do. the notification sends a report of the engages, friends, discoveries made through whoosh during the period of time chosen. Notifications also send for events or when exclusive content is being sent. When something is happening on Whoosh you don’t really need to wait for your notification to be notified but the icon of the app will shine blue like some apps did in the Wii U to alert the user that new engages were made in the app."*
